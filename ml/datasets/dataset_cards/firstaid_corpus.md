# IshVenom First-Aid Corpus

Multilingual instruction-tuning corpus for snakebite first-aid generation.
Used to fine-tune Gemma 4 E2B for the IshVenom mobile app.

## ⚠️ Medical safety notice

**This corpus contains medical first-aid content and must be handled with
extreme care.** It is NOT a substitute for clinical training or professional
medical care. The model fine-tuned on this corpus is intended to support —
not replace — community health workers and emergency responders.

## Construction

1. **30 canonical English instructions** authored by the project lead,
   paraphrased from WHO snakebite guidelines (no verbatim reproduction).
2. **French translations** authored by the project lead (draft, needs native
   speaker and clinical review).
3. **Arabic, Hausa, Swahili, Twi** — placeholder stubs. The synthetic
   generator **refuses to emit training data** until these are filled in by
   qualified human translators AND reviewed by a clinician.
4. **~5,000 synthetic training examples** generated by pairing each gold
   instruction with realistic community health worker prompts across 20
   priority snake species, optionally re-phrased using linguistic patterns
   pulled from AfriSenti, MasakhaNEWS, and XL-Sum (the "corpus grounding"
   trick from Jane Odum's EpiCast submission).

## Verification workflow

Every language file tracks a `verification_status`:
- `stub_needs_full_human_translation`
- `draft_needs_native_speaker_and_clinical_review`
- `authored_by_project_lead_needs_clinical_review`
- `native_speaker_reviewed_needs_clinical_review`
- `fully_verified`

The `verify_gold.py` script gates fine-tuning. No model will be trained on a
corpus that fails verification for the `fine_tuning` safety tier.

## Covered topics

- Prevention (5 instructions)
- Symptom identification (5)
- Immediate response (5)
- What NEVER to do (5)
- Transport to clinic (5)
- Special cases: children, pregnancy, eye exposure, face bites, anaphylaxis (5)

## Not intended for

- Teaching first aid without a human instructor
- Clinical decision-making
- Use by anyone who cannot verify the output language fluently
